How it works

  1. Fill the formEnter your details carefully.

  3. VerificationVisit the college with original documents.

Documents required

  • 10th & 12th marksheets
  • Transfer certificate (TC)
  • Migration certificate
  • Caste / EWS certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhaar card
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • Samagra ID
Your details are not stored on this website. They go only to the college WhatsApp number, and only after you press Send.
Questions

Admission FAQs

Is the form free?
The online enquiry form is free. Official admission fees are paid at the college office as per the Department of Higher Education norms.
Do I have to visit the college?
Yes. After you send the form, bring original documents to the office for verification and fee deposit.
Can I choose two courses?
You can mention a second preference in the form. Seats are allotted by merit and as per reservation rules.
