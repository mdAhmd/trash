Steps

How to check

  1. Click “Check result”You will be taken to the official result page.
  2. Select your examChoose course, semester and year.
  3. Enter enrolment numberUse the number printed on your admit card.
  4. DownloadSave or print the result for your records. Original marksheets are issued later.
Questions

Result FAQs

When will results be declared?
Results are declared by the affiliated university after the exams. Dates are announced on the university website and the college notice board.
What if my name or marks are wrong?
Contact the college examination cell within 7 days with your ID card and a photocopy of your marksheet. We will forward the correction to the university.
How do I apply for re-checking or re-evaluation?
Applications are made through the college exam cell within the date announced by the university, with the prescribed fee.
When can I collect my original marksheet?
Original marksheets are issued by the university. The college office informs students when they arrive.
